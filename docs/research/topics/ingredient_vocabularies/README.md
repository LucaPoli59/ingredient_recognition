# Ingredient vocabularies

**Created:** 2026-08-04  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-04

This topic records general research on controlled vocabularies, ontologies, thesauri, and food databases that may support canonical ingredient naming. It is intentionally independent of one dataset so it can inform later data integration, ingredient selection, evaluation, and thesis writing.

## Scope

The research asks which resources provide reusable food or ingredient concepts, stable identifiers, labels and synonyms, hierarchy or facet information, downloadable releases, and deterministic offline use. It also distinguishes an ingredient vocabulary from adjacent resources designed for nutritional records, food-exposure coding, retail products, or general knowledge representation.

## Files

- [`vocabulary_catalog.md`](vocabulary_catalog.md) is the reusable, dataset-independent catalog of the principal resources investigated.
