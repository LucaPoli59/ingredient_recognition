# Third-party guidance and attribution

This project guide uses the following ideas from [K-Dense Scientific Agent Skills](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/tree/593377bb048752abb0152deab9fbb1083a6087df), reviewed on 2026-10-21:

- `scientific-writing`: separate drafting from verification and bind claims to exact evidence.
- `literature-review`: record searches and synthesize thematically with explicit coverage limits.
- `peer-review`: connect a criticism to a manuscript location and evidence; distinguish missing reporting from a demonstrated inconsistency.

These three skills are design references, not installed dependencies. The local instructions are adapted to the author's English Markdown thesis and existing repository authorities. Their clinical/journal intake gates, compulsory registries, fixed review structures and external-service routes are not adopted.

`citation-management` and `scientific-visualization` are installed unmodified specialists at the revision in [skill_sources.json](skill_sources.json). Their complete upstream license is preserved in [THIRD_PARTY_LICENSE.txt](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSE.txt) and in each installed package.

Library reference: Timothy Kassis, Vinayak Agarwal, Yuhuan He, Darshil Patel, and Aubrey M. Brueckner (2026), [Scientific Agent Skills: A Library of Procedural Knowledge for Research Agents](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.00065). The arXiv record was checked on 2026-10-21 (then version 2; no journal reference displayed). This is software/workflow attribution, not evidence for ingredient-recognition results. It is recorded here and in the setup report; no thesis bibliography has yet been created.
